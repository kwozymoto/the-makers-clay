shantell-mark.woff2 is Shantell Sans, subset to the eleven letters used in the
wordmark ("the makers clay"). Shantell Sans is licensed under the SIL Open Font
License 1.1 — the full licence is in OFL-ShantellSans.txt in this folder.

Subsetting and redistribution are permitted under the OFL. The font is not sold
and is not used as a standalone font product.

Regenerate with `npm run font:mark` from the project root.

Fraunces (used for headings) is also OFL 1.1 and ships via npm; its licence is
at node_modules/@fontsource-variable/fraunces/LICENSE.
